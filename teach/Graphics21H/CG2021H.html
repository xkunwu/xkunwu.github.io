Course Information

Short Intro

The objective of this course is to provide a complete introduction to computer graphics and from several perspectives1:

  • its applications, the various fields that are involved in the study of graphics,
  • some of the tools that make the images produced by graphics so effective,
  • some numbers to help you understand the scales at which computer graphics works,
  • elementary ideas required to write your graphics programs.

Description

The course aims at providing foundamental knowledge and existing techniques of computer graphics related topics.

The course will touch on the following topics:

  • What is color? And how human perceive color?
  • How to store color information in an image?
  • How is 3D geometry rendered into 2D image?
  • How to model 3D world with computer?
  • How to make artificial image looks real?
  • How to animate figures?

Keywords: computer graphics

Prerequisites

Required：

  • Algebra: vector, matrix computation, Euclidean spaces.
  • Calculus: Jacobian, Hessian, chain rule.
  • Geometry: curve, surface, curvature.
  • Physics: light, color.
  • Data structure: sequential & random access.
  • Programming (object-oriented): C++/C#/Java/Python (at least one proficient).
  • Computer architecture: parallel architecture.

Recommended：

  • Statistics: probability density, sampling.
  • Numerical optimization: notion of minima, gradient descent.
  • Algorithm analysis: computational costs.
  • Visual computing: image processing.
  • Signal processing: Fourier transform.
  • Physics: mechanics, optics.
  • Aesthetics: color, light & shadow.
